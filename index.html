A bit about me

I graduated from the University of South Florida in December 2025 with a degree in Computer Science, and I spent both of my internship summers at Dell working on genuinely different sides of engineering — one summer shipping features for an enterprise platform, the next building an AI system from scratch. That second summer is what pulled me toward wanting to keep working on AI as a real part of how I build, not a feature bolted on afterward.

I also spent a few months on the product side at an early-stage AI startup, which changed how I think about what I build — it's not just does the code work, it's does this actually work for the person using it. Outside of internships, I like finding something that's clearly broken and just building the fix — that's where most of my side projects come from.
